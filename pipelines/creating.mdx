This page reflects the current app flow, not an older all-in-one wizard. In practice, you create the shell first and then finish configuration in the builder.

## Step by step

1. Create and test the source and destination connections from **Connections**.
2. Go to **Data Pipelines**, click **+ New Pipeline**, give it a clear name, choose the source connection, and click **Create & open canvas**.
3. In **Source Configuration**, pick the exact table or resource to sync, such as `public.orders`, `orders`, `deals`, or `pull_requests`.
4. Set the sync mode that matches the source: full, incremental, or PostgreSQL CDC.
5. Keep the first transform simple. A light rename, cast, or derived column is easier to validate than a large rewrite.
6. Open **Destination**, choose the destination connection, set the target `Schema` and `Table`, and keep `Write mode` on `Upsert`.
7. Save the pipeline.
8. Click **Run Now** and inspect **Run History**.
9. Verify the destination rows before adding a recurring schedule.

## A realistic first pipeline

A good first production rollout is:

- source connection: `Shopify Production`
- selected resource: `orders`
- transform: normalize currency codes and add `loaded_from`
- destination: `Analytics Postgres`
- target table: `raw_shopify_orders`
- cadence: hourly after one successful manual run

That sequence exercises the full live app flow without introducing unnecessary risk on day one.
