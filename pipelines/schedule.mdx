---
title: Schedule and Automation
description: Configure automatic sync intervals for pipelines.
---

You can run pipelines manually at any time, or schedule them to run automatically at intervals you define.

## Manual Runs

Click **Run** on a pipeline to execute it immediately. The run is queued and processed by the ETL service. You can monitor the run status in the run history.

## Scheduled Runs

Configure a schedule to run the pipeline automatically. Common intervals:

- Every 15 minutes
- Every hour
- Every 6 hours
- Daily (e.g. at 2:00 AM)
- Weekly (e.g. Sunday at midnight)

The schedule is stored and executed by the platform. The minimum interval depends on your plan.

## Pause and Resume

You can pause a pipeline to stop scheduled runs. Manual runs may still be allowed depending on configuration. Resume the pipeline to re-enable the schedule.
