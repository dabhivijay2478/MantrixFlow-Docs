MantrixFlow offers Free, Plus, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Pipeline capacity and monthly delivered-row allowances are enforced by the application.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual price | Pipelines | Delivered rows per month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | `$0` | `$0` | `5` | `25,000` |
| Plus | `$29` | `$22/month`, billed annually | `20` | `1,000,000` |
| Pro | `$129` | `$97/month`, billed annually | `50` | `10,000,000` |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Contact sales | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Note>
  The hosted checkout shows the final price, billing period, payment methods,
  taxes, and any applicable discounts before you confirm a paid plan.
</Note>

## Free

New workspaces start on Free without a payment step. Free includes up to five pipelines and 25,000 delivered rows per month.

## Plus

Plus includes up to 20 pipelines and 1,000,000 delivered rows per month. Start checkout from the billing page in the application.

## Pro

Pro includes up to 50 pipelines and 10,000,000 delivered rows per month. Start checkout from the billing page in the application.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing and requirements are handled through sales. Contact [support@mantrixflow.com](mailto:support@mantrixflow.com) for current terms.

## When you reach a limit

- You cannot create another pipeline after reaching the pipeline limit.
- New pipeline runs pause after the workspace reaches its monthly delivered-row limit.
- Upgrade the plan or wait for the monthly row allowance to reset.

The application shows current usage and the next available plan. See [Billing and usage](/billing-breakdown) for checkout, plan changes, and limit behavior.
