MANTrixFlow is a data integration platform that syncs data from your sources to your destinations. You connect your data sources (databases, SaaS apps, cloud warehouses), configure pipelines that define what to sync and how, and run syncs on demand or on a schedule.

## What You Need

- A MANTrixFlow account (sign up at [cloud.mantrixflow.com](https://cloud.mantrixflow.com))
- Access to at least one data source you want to sync from
- A destination where you want the data to land (e.g. a database, warehouse, or data lake)

## How It Works

1. **Create connections** — Add your source and destination credentials. Credentials are encrypted and stored securely.
2. **Create a pipeline** — Link a source connection to a destination connection. Choose which streams (tables or data feeds) to sync.
3. **Configure sync** — Set the sync mode (full, incremental, or CDC) and write mode (append, replace, or upsert).
4. **Run** — Execute the pipeline manually or on a schedule. Monitor run history and sync state.
